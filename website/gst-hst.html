GST/HST

GST/HST Bookkeeping in Toronto

Your GST/HST return prepared and filed from books that are already reconciled.

GST/HST return preparation and filing are included in your monthly scope. We keep your receipts and records organized against the Canada Revenue Agency's ITC documentation bands through the year, so your return rests on documents that already meet the rules.

The ITC bands, in summary

Under $100

The supplier's name, the invoice date (or the date tax was paid), and the total paid are enough.

$100 or more, and less than $500

The same, plus the supplier's GST/HST registration number and the tax details set out in the Regulations.

$500 or more

Everything in the lower bands, plus your name as recipient, the payment terms, and a description of each supply.

The ITC bands, in full

Under the GST/HST rules (Regulations SOR-91-45), the information a supporting document must contain depends on the total amount paid or payable:

Under $100

The name of the supplier or the intermediary, or the name under which the supplier or the intermediary does business; where an invoice is issued, the date of the invoice; where an invoice is not issued, the date on which there is tax paid or payable; and the total amount paid or payable for all of the supplies. (SOR-91-45, s. 3(a))

$100 or more, and less than $500

Everything in the under-$100 band, except that the supplier's or intermediary's name must be given together with the registration number assigned to the supplier or the intermediary, plus the following, as they apply:

  • Where the amount paid or payable does not include the tax: the amount of tax paid or payable for each supply, or for all of the supplies — or, where provincial sales tax applies as set out in the Regulations, the combined tax totals with a statement that the total includes the tax paid or payable under Division II of Part IX of the Excise Tax Act.
  • Where the amount includes the tax and one or more supplies are taxable and not zero-rated: a statement that tax is included in the amount for each taxable supply, the total tax rate, and the amount paid or payable for each such supply, or for all such supplies to which the same total tax rate applies.
  • Where the status of two or more supplies is different: an indication of the status of each taxable supply that is not a zero-rated supply.

(SOR-91-45, s. 3(b))

$500 or more

Everything in the bands above (s. 3(a) and s. 3(b)), plus the recipient's name, the name under which the recipient does business, or the name of the recipient's duly authorized agent or representative; the terms of payment; and a description of each supply sufficient to identify it. (SOR-91-45, s. 3(c))
