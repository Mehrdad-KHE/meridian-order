GST/HST

GST/HST return preparation and filing are included in your monthly scope, on the Canada Revenue Agency's own rules — including what a receipt must show before input tax credits (ITCs) can be claimed.

More on GST/HST bookkeeping in Toronto

The ITC bands, in plain language

Under $100: the supplier's name, the invoice date (or the date tax was paid), and the total paid are enough.
$100 or more, and less than $500: the same, plus the supplier's GST/HST registration number and the tax details set out in the Regulations.
$500 or more: everything in the lower bands, plus your name as recipient, the payment terms, and a description of each supply.

We keep your receipts and records organized against these bands through the year, so claiming ITCs at filing time rests on documents that already meet the rules. GST/HST return preparation and filing are included in your monthly scope — see the scope line on the Pricing page.
