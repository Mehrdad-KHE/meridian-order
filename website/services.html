Services

Bookkeeping services for Toronto small businesses

The full bookkeeping cycle, handled remotely for one fixed monthly fee — from $550/month + HST, month-to-month. Every service below is part of that cycle; catch-up work is quoted separately, as a fixed fee for a defined scope.

01

Full-cycle bookkeeping

Day-to-day recording, categorization, and upkeep of your books in QuickBooks, Xero or Sage 50, kept current on a steady monthly rhythm instead of a year-end scramble. Every entry is tied to its source document — a statement, receipt or invoice — so each number in your books can be traced and defended.

02

Accounts payable & receivable

Bills tracked and paid on time, invoices issued and followed up — so you always know what you owe and what you are owed, and nothing ages quietly in a drawer.

03

Bank & credit-card reconciliations

Every account reconciled to its statement each month. Discrepancies are found and fixed early, while they are still small — this is the control that makes the rest of the numbers trustworthy.

04

Catch-up & cleanup

Months — or years — behind? Backlogged books are brought current methodically. It starts with the free intro call and review; you receive a clear plan and a fixed quote for a defined scope before any work begins. No judgement, no surprises.

05

Payroll support

Payroll entries, source-deduction tracking, and organized records that keep paydays smooth and filings straightforward.

06

GST/HST preparation support

Sales tax tracked through the month so your return can be prepared from reconciled figures, not reconstructed at the deadline. Receipts and invoices are checked for the information CRA requires to support input tax credits; the prescribed information depends on the purchase amount — under $100, $100 to $500, and $500 or more (Input Tax Credit Information (GST/HST) Regulations).

07

Monthly reporting

Straightforward monthly reports from your accounting system, plus an Excel workbook: where the money came from, where it went, and where the business stands — in plain language.

08

Year-end file for your accountant

Meridian Books provides bookkeeping services; tax filing stays with your own accountant. At year-end they receive a reconciled, complete, documented file — reconciled accounts and organized records — prepared for them, which makes their work faster and your filing smoother.

Pricing

Ongoing monthly bookkeeping is a fixed monthly fee — from $550/month + HST — set after the free intro call and review, based on the volume and complexity of your file. The fee stays fixed month to month; the arrangement is month-to-month and you can cancel any time. Your books stay in your own accounting system, so leaving never means losing your file.

Catch-up and cleanup of backlogged books is quoted separately, as a fixed fee for a defined scope, before the work begins.
