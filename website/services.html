Monthly Bookkeeping

Close and reconcile your books every month — so you always know where your business stands.

Our position: books are a monthly habit, not a year-end event. A month that is closed is a month you can make decisions from.

Included

  • Reconcile your bank and credit-card accounts every month
  • Record and categorize your receipts, invoices, and statements
  • Send you a short monthly summary of what was done and anything needing a decision
  • Keep your file in your own QuickBooks, Xero, or Sage 50
  • Prepare and file your GST/HST returns

Not included

Tax filing and tax advice — year-end is prepared for your own accountant instead (see below).

Price and terms

From $550/month + HST, month-to-month. The scope the fee covers is printed beside the price on the Pricing page. Your month is closed and reported by the 15th of the following month.

Catch-Up & Cleanup

Bring a backlog of months — or longer — fully up to date and reconciled.

Our position: a backlog is a finite project with a fixed price agreed in advance — never an open meter running against you.

Included

  • Review your backlog on the free intro call
  • Convert and reconcile the backlog months
  • Agree a defined scope in writing before work starts
  • Hand over cleanly into monthly bookkeeping, if you continue

Not included

Work outside the defined scope agreed in the quote — anything extra is quoted before it is done, never added after.

Price and terms

A fixed fee for a defined scope, quoted before work starts. As a guide, one year of catch-up typically falls between $1,800 and $2,400 — see the Catch-Up & Cleanup page. How catch-up bookkeeping works

Payroll Support

Record every pay run properly in your books, so payroll never becomes a year-end problem.

Our position: payroll belongs inside the monthly books, recorded when it happens — not reconstructed in a panic at year-end.

Included

Not included

Included in the standard monthly scope — payroll for up to 5 employees — within the fixed monthly retainer from $550/month + HST.

Year-End Preparation for Your Accountant

Give your own accountant a reconciled, organized year-end file — not a box of loose documents.

Our position: your accountant should receive a file, not a problem. Preparation is our work; filing stays theirs.

Included

GST/HST

Prepare and file your GST/HST returns on the Canada Revenue Agency's own rules — including what a receipt must show before input tax credits (ITCs) can be claimed.

Our position: a return is only as strong as the receipts behind it — documents first, then the filing.

Included

  • Prepare and file your GST/HST returns as part of your monthly scope
  • Keep your receipts and records organized against the ITC bands through the year
  • Keep the documents behind every claim on file

Price and terms

Included in your monthly scope — see the scope line on the Pricing page.

More on GST/HST bookkeeping in Toronto

The ITC bands, in plain language

Under $100

The supplier's name, the invoice date (or the date tax was paid), and the total paid are enough.

$100 or more, and less than $500

The same, plus the supplier's GST/HST registration number and the tax details set out in the Regulations.

$500 or more

Everything in the lower bands, plus your name as recipient, the payment terms, and a description of each supply.
