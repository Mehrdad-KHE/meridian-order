GST/HST

GST/HST is handled as part of your monthly books, on the Canada Revenue Agency's own rules — including what a receipt must show before input tax credits (ITCs) can be claimed.

Under the GST/HST rules (Regulations SOR-91-45), the information a supporting document must contain depends on the total amount paid or payable:

Total amountWhat the document must show
Under $100The supplier's name or business name, the date of the invoice (or, where there is no invoice, the date tax was paid or payable), and the total amount paid or payable.
$100 to $500The items for under $100, plus the supplier's business number, the buyer's name or business name, the payment terms, and a statement showing which items are taxed and which are not.
$500 or moreThe items for the $100 to $500 band, plus a breakdown of the consideration for each item, the tax paid or payable for each item, and the place where each item was delivered or made available.

Meridian keeps your receipts and records organized against these bands through the year, so claiming ITCs at filing time rests on documents that already meet the rules. Whether GST/HST filing itself is inside your monthly scope is stated in writing in that scope — tracking is part of the monthly books either way.
