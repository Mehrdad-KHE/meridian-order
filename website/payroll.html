Payroll Support

Payroll Support for Toronto Small Businesses

Pay runs recorded properly in your books, so payroll never becomes a year-end problem.

What is included

  • Pay runs recorded in your books as part of the monthly cycle
  • Payroll amounts reconciled against your bank account
  • Source deductions tracked in your books through the year
  • T4-season support for your books: payroll records organized and reconciled for the year
  • Payroll for up to 5 employees is included in the standard monthly scope

Boundaries

Payroll support at Meridian Books is bookkeeping support: we record and reconcile your pay runs in your own QuickBooks, Xero, or Sage 50 file. It is not tax advice. Decisions about pay, deductions, and filings rest with you and your own accountant.

Price and terms

Included in the monthly scope: from $550/month + HST, month-to-month. Covers up to 100 transactions a month, up to 3 bank and credit-card accounts, monthly reconciliation and reports, GST/HST return preparation and filing, and payroll for up to 5 employees. Above that scope, the fee is adjusted in writing before any work begins.

Your month is closed and reported by the 15th of the following month.
