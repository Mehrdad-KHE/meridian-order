About Meridian Books

A Toronto bookkeeping practice with one person accountable for your file: the founder. Senior, documented, and continuous — not a stopgap.

Meridian Books is founded and led by Mehrdad Kheirollahi, a bookkeeper with more than two decades of accounting and bookkeeping experience.

That experience shapes how the practice works: books are kept the way an accountant would want to receive them — every transaction recorded, every account reconciled, every deadline tracked. When something does not balance, it gets found and fixed, not carried forward.

Mehrdad leads every client file personally. The same senior professional who reviews your books at the start is the one who closes them each month, so the knowledge of your business compounds instead of resetting with every new hire.

The standard is simple: a small business owner should be able to ask “where do we stand?” at any moment — and get a straight, current answer from their own books.

The short answers

Who is Meridian Books?

A Toronto bookkeeping practice founded and led personally by Mehrdad Kheirollahi, a bookkeeper with 20+ years of accounting and bookkeeping experience.

What does Meridian Books do?

Bookkeeping services: full-cycle bookkeeping, bank and credit-card reconciliations, catch-up and cleanup, payroll support, GST/HST preparation support, monthly reporting, and a year-end file prepared for your own accountant.

Who is it for?

Small businesses that need their books kept current every month — including businesses whose books are months behind and need a methodical catch-up first.

Where does Meridian Books work?

Based in Toronto, Ontario, and fully remote — serving small businesses across Canada in their own time zones.

Why trust Meridian Books?

One senior bookkeeper is personally accountable for your file, every entry is tied to a source document, every account is reconciled monthly, and deadlines are agreed in writing in advance.

How do you start?

With a free intro call. We review where your books stand, you receive a clear plan and a fixed-fee quote, and the arrangement is month-to-month from $550/month + HST.
