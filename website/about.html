About Meridian Books

Who Meridian Books is: a Toronto bookkeeping practice, a named founder, and a method you can check.

Founder

Mehrdad Kheirollahi

Founder & Bookkeeper

Mehrdad Kheirollahi is a Toronto bookkeeper with more than 20 years of experience in accounting and business work, including bookkeeping, payroll, purchasing, inventory, and budgeting for the businesses he has run and worked in.

He founded Meridian Books as an Ontario sole proprietorship to offer small businesses one thing, done properly: bookkeeping that starts from your documents as they are, and ends with reconciled books you can rely on.

The practice is deliberately personal. The person you speak to on the intro call is the person who keeps your books.

Method

How Meridian works

Documents in, import-ready reconciled books out — in your own software.

You send your bank statements, receipts, and invoices as they are. Meridian records and categorizes them, reconciles every bank and credit-card account, and closes the month in your own QuickBooks, Xero, or Sage 50 file. You receive a short monthly summary of what was done and anything that needs your decision. Nothing is asked of you beyond the documents themselves — no sorting homework, no spreadsheets to fill in first.
