Before the call

What should I prepare for the intro call?

Nothing formal. If you can say roughly how many months your books cover, which accounts you use, and what software you are on, that is plenty — the review starts from there.

How do I send my documents securely?

After the intro call you are given a secure way to send statements, receipts, and invoices. Never send passwords, bank credentials, or tax documents through the contact form on this site.

How soon can work start?

Once your scope is agreed in writing. Monthly bookkeeping starts with the current month; catch-up work starts with the backlog scope quoted on your intro call.
