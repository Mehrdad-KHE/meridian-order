Privacy

Privacy

What this site collects, what it never collects, and how client documents are handled — in plain language.

What is collected through this site

When you use the contact form or email Meridian Books, you provide your name, email address, business name, and the contents of your message. That information is used only to respond to you and, if you become a client, to provide bookkeeping services.

What is never collected

Meridian Books never asks for passwords, bank credentials, or tax documents through this website or its contact form. Do not send them. Client documents are exchanged only by the method arranged directly with you after your intro call — never through this website or its form.

How client documents are handled

Statements, receipts, and invoices you provide are used only for your bookkeeping. They are not sold, shared with marketers, or used for any other client's work; disclosure happens only where the law requires it.

PIPEDA

Meridian Books handles personal information under the principles of Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Information is collected for the purposes stated on this page, used only for those purposes, and safeguarded as described here — within Meridian, only the founder handles client documents. You may ask to see the personal information held about you, or to have it corrected, at info@meridianbooks.ca.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Hosting logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses as host for security and operational purposes. Meridian Books does not receive or use those logs for marketing.

Fonts

Fonts are served from this site; no third party receives your visit data from us.

Retention

Enquiry messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the conversation. Client records are retained as needed for the bookkeeping work and applicable record-keeping obligations, and your own QuickBooks, Xero, or Sage 50 file remains yours.
