Privacy policy

How Meridian Books handles information, in plain language. Last updated: November 2026.

What we receive

When you contact Meridian Books, we receive what you send us: your name, email address, phone number, and the details you share about your business and your books. As a client, you also provide business financial records — statements, receipts, invoices and access to your accounting system — so the bookkeeping can be done.

How it is used

Information is used only to respond to your enquiry, to provide the bookkeeping services described in your engagement letter, and to meet legal record-keeping obligations. It is not sold, and it is not used for unrelated marketing.

Who can see it

Your file is handled personally by the founder of Meridian Books. Records are shared only where the service requires it — for example, with your own accountant at year-end, with your direction — or where the law requires disclosure.

Your records stay yours

Your books live in your own accounting system. If the arrangement ends, your file and records remain yours.

Questions
