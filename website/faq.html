Working together

Working together

Who does the work?

Mehrdad Kheirollahi, the founder. The person you speak to on the intro call is the person who keeps your books. See the founder profile on the About page.

Which software do you work in?

QuickBooks, Xero, and Sage 50, with Excel where it helps. Work is done in your own file, so your books stay in your name and under your control. See monthly bookkeeping services and the software they are kept in.

Do you help with payroll?

Yes — payroll for up to 5 employees is included in the standard monthly scope: pay runs recorded, source deductions tracked, and T4-season support for your books. See payroll support for Toronto small businesses. Payroll bookkeeping support is not tax advice.

How do I send my documents?

Securely, after the intro call — statements, receipts, and invoices as they are. No sorting or spreadsheet homework first. Never send passwords or bank credentials. See how documents are handled on the Privacy page.

My books are behind. Where do I start?

With the free intro call. Your backlog is reviewed, then quoted as one fixed fee for a defined scope, agreed in writing before work starts. See catch-up bookkeeping for how it works.

Money and terms

Money and terms

How much does monthly bookkeeping cost?

A fixed retainer from $550/month + HST. Covers up to 100 transactions a month, up to 3 bank and credit-card accounts, monthly reconciliation and reports, GST/HST return preparation and filing, and payroll for up to 5 employees. Above that scope, the fee is adjusted in writing before any work begins. See bookkeeping pricing for the full breakdown.

Is it month-to-month?

Yes. There is no long-term contract. See month-to-month pricing and what the fee covers.

What does catch-up cost?

A fixed fee for a defined scope, quoted after the free backlog review and agreed in writing before any work starts. As a guide, one year of catch-up typically falls between $1,800 and $2,400 and takes about 2–4 weeks — see Catch-Up & Cleanup.

Do you file my taxes?

No. Meridian Books is a bookkeeping practice, not a tax filer. Year-end work is prepared as a reconciled file for your own accountant, who files your taxes. See year-end preparation for your accountant.

Documents and software

Your documents and software

Are my documents kept private?

Yes. Documents are used only to do your bookkeeping, handled as described on the Privacy page on document handling and retention, and never sent anywhere else. Passwords and bank credentials are never asked for and should never be sent.

What happens at year-end?

Your accounts are reconciled for the full year and an organized file is prepared for your own accountant, so filing starts from books that are already in order. See year-end preparation services for your accountant.

Can you handle GST/HST?

Yes. GST/HST return preparation and filing are included in your monthly scope, and your receipts are kept organized against the Canada Revenue Agency's ITC documentation bands (under $100, $100 to $500, and $500 or more). See GST/HST bookkeeping in Toronto.
