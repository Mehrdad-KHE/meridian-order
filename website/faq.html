FAQ

Bookkeeping questions, answered plainly

Short questions, direct answers. Anything else — ask on a free intro call.

How does remote bookkeeping actually work?
Your records live in your own accounting system — QuickBooks Online or Desktop, Xero, or Sage 50 — and shared, organized files. Statements, receipts, and invoices flow in digitally; reconciliations, reports, and deadlines are handled on a monthly rhythm. You get the same rigour as an in-house bookkeeper, without the desk, payroll, or supervision.
My books are months behind. Can you still help?
Yes — catch-up and cleanup is a core service. It starts with the free intro call and review: we assess how far behind the books are, then give you a clear plan and a fixed quote for a defined scope before any work begins. No judgement, no surprises.
What does the free intro call involve?
A look at your current bookkeeping file and records: what is up to date, what is missing, and what needs cleanup. You receive an honest assessment and a clear plan with pricing. There is no obligation to continue, and no work starts until you approve the plan and sign a simple engagement letter.
How is pricing structured?
Ongoing monthly bookkeeping is a fixed monthly fee, from $550/month + HST, set after the free intro call and review based on the volume and complexity of your file. It is month-to-month and you can cancel any time. Catch-up and cleanup of backlogged books is quoted separately, as a fixed fee for a defined scope.
Do you file my taxes?
No. Meridian Books provides bookkeeping services. At year-end, your own accountant receives a reconciled, complete, documented file prepared for them, which makes their work faster and your filing smoother. GST/HST figures are prepared from your reconciled books as part of the monthly cycle.
What receipts do I need to keep for GST/HST input tax credits?
Keep the receipt or invoice for every business purchase. Under the Input Tax Credit Information (GST/HST) Regulations, the information a supporting document must show depends on the purchase amount: under $100, $100 to $500, and $500 or more — larger purchases need more detail on the document, such as the supplier's GST/HST number. As part of your monthly bookkeeping, documents are checked against these bands so a missing detail is flagged while it can still be fixed.
Do you work with my accountant?
Yes. Your books are kept the way an accountant would want to receive them, and at year-end your accountant receives a reconciled, complete, documented file — reconciled accounts and organized records.
Where do you work, and who will handle my file?
Meridian Books is based in Toronto and works remotely with small businesses across Canada. Every file is led personally by the founder, a bookkeeper with 20+ years of accounting and bookkeeping experience.
Can I cancel?
Yes. The arrangement is month-to-month and you can cancel any time. Your books stay in your own accounting system, so leaving never means losing your file.
