Working together

Working together

Who does the work?

Mehrdad Kheirollahi, the founder. The person you speak to on the intro call is the person who keeps your books.

Which software do you work in?

QuickBooks, Xero, and Sage 50, with Excel where it helps. Work is done in your own file, so your books stay in your name and under your control.

How do I send my documents?

Securely, after the intro call — statements, receipts, and invoices as they are. No sorting or spreadsheet homework first. Never send passwords or bank credentials.

My books are behind. Where do I start?

With the free intro call. Your backlog is reviewed, then quoted as one fixed fee for a defined scope, agreed in writing before work starts.

Money and terms

Money and terms

How much does monthly bookkeeping cost?

A fixed retainer from $550/month + HST. The scope it covers — transaction volume, bank and credit-card accounts, GST/HST handling, and payroll where included — is printed beside the price on the Pricing page and confirmed in writing before you start.

Is it month-to-month?

Yes. There is no long-term contract.

What does catch-up cost?

A fixed fee for a defined scope, quoted after the free backlog review and agreed in writing before any work starts.

Do you file my taxes?

No. Meridian Books is a bookkeeping practice, not a tax filer. Year-end work is prepared as a reconciled file for your own accountant, who files your taxes.

Documents and software

Your documents and software

Are my documents kept private?

Yes. Documents are used only to do your bookkeeping, handled as described on the Privacy page, and never sent anywhere else. Passwords and bank credentials are never asked for and should never be sent.

What happens at year-end?

Your accounts are reconciled for the full year and an organized file is prepared for your own accountant, so filing starts from books that are already in order.

Can you handle GST/HST?

GST/HST handling is part of the monthly books, and your receipts are kept organized against the Canada Revenue Agency's ITC documentation bands (under $100, $100 to $500, and $500 or more). Whether filing itself is inside your scope is stated in writing in that scope.
