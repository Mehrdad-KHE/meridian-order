The basis on which Meridian Books provides bookkeeping services. Last updated: November 2026.

Services

Meridian Books provides bookkeeping services: full-cycle bookkeeping, reconciliations, catch-up and cleanup, payroll support, GST/HST preparation support, monthly reporting, and a year-end file prepared for your own accountant. Meridian Books does not file income taxes; tax filing remains with your own accountant.

Engagement first

No work begins until the scope and fee are agreed in a written engagement letter signed by both sides. The free intro call and review carry no obligation.

Fees

Ongoing monthly bookkeeping is charged at a fixed monthly fee, from $550/month + HST, set in the engagement letter for your file. The arrangement is month-to-month and either side may end it; your books remain in your own accounting system. Catch-up and cleanup work is quoted separately, as a fixed fee for a defined scope, before it begins.

Your responsibilities

Bookkeeping can only be as complete as the records behind it. Clients provide statements, receipts and invoices, and access to their accounting system, on the agreed monthly rhythm.

Website content

Content on this website is general information about the practice and its services. It is not accounting, tax or legal advice for your specific situation.
