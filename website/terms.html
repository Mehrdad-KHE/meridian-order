Terms

The terms Meridian Books works on — in plain language.

The service

Meridian Books is a bookkeeping practice. The service is bookkeeping: recording and categorizing transactions, reconciling bank and credit-card accounts, handling GST/HST as set out in an agreed scope, and preparing year-end files for the client's own accountant. Meridian Books does not provide audit services, tax filing, or tax advice.

Engagement terms

Monthly bookkeeping is a fixed monthly retainer from $550/month + HST, month-to-month, for the scope agreed in writing before work starts. Catch-up and cleanup work is charged at a fixed fee for a defined scope, quoted and agreed in writing before work starts. Work outside an agreed scope is quoted before it is done.

Client responsibilities

Clients provide their statements, receipts, and invoices, keep their software subscriptions in their own name, and remain responsible for decisions about their business and for filings made by their own accountant.

Liability

Meridian Books takes care to keep accurate, reconciled books from the documents provided. Its responsibility is limited to the bookkeeping work described in the agreed scope; it is not responsible for information that was not provided, or for tax filings and advice, which rest with the client and the client's own accountant.
