FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

How does remote bookkeeping actually work?
Your records live in your own accounting system — QuickBooks Online or Desktop, Xero, or Sage 50 — and shared, organized files. Statements, receipts, and invoices flow in digitally; reconciliations, reports, and deadlines are handled on a monthly rhythm. You get the same rigour as an in-house bookkeeper, without the desk, payroll, or supervision.
My books are months behind. Can you still help?
Yes — catch-up and cleanup is a core service. It starts with the free intro call and review: we assess how far behind the books are, then give you a clear plan and a fixed quote for bringing them current before any work begins. No judgement, no surprises.
How is pricing structured?
Ongoing monthly bookkeeping is a fixed monthly fee, from $550/month + HST, set after the free intro call and review based on the volume and complexity of your file. It is month-to-month and you can cancel any time. Catch-up and cleanup of backlogged books is quoted separately, as a fixed fee for a defined scope.
Do you work with my accountant?
Yes. At year-end your accountant receives a reconciled, complete, documented file — reconciled accounts and organized records — which makes their work faster and your filing smoother.
