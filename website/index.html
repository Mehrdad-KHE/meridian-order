Questions

Before you call

How much does it cost?

Monthly bookkeeping is a fixed retainer from $550/month + HST, month-to-month. The scope the fee covers is printed on the Pricing page, beside the price.

Do you file my taxes?

No. Meridian Books is a bookkeeping practice. Year-end work is prepared as a reconciled file for your own accountant, who files your taxes.

My books are months behind. Can you help?

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup is a separate service: your backlog is reviewed on the free intro call, then quoted as one fixed fee for a defined scope before any work starts.

Which software do you work in?

QuickBooks, Xero, and Sage 50, with Excel where it helps. The work is done in your own file, so your books stay yours.
