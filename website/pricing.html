Pricing questions

The money questions, answered

What does the $550/month + HST cover?

Covers up to 100 transactions a month, up to 3 bank and credit-card accounts, monthly reconciliation and reports, GST/HST return preparation and filing, and payroll for up to 5 employees. Above that scope, the fee is adjusted in writing before any work begins.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. The retainer is month-to-month.

What does catch-up cost?

Catch-up is a fixed fee for a defined scope. Your backlog is reviewed on the free intro call, and the full fee is quoted and agreed in writing before any work starts. As a guide, one year of catch-up typically falls between $1,800 and $2,400 and takes about 2–4 weeks — see Catch-Up & Cleanup.

Are there extra charges I should expect?

No work outside your agreed scope is done — or charged — without a quote you approve first. Software subscriptions for your own QuickBooks, Xero, or Sage 50 file stay in your name.

Do you file my taxes?

No. Year-end is prepared as a reconciled file for your own accountant, who files your taxes.
