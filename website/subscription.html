How it works

Three steps

  1. Send your documents. Statements, receipts, invoices — as they are.
  2. Get the structured file. Your documents come back as one import-ready file, with the sources in order.
  3. Import and review. The file goes into your own QuickBooks, Xero or Sage 50; you review and keep your books your way.
Scope

What the subscription is — and is not

Included

Document conversion: extraction and structuring of statements, receipts and invoices into an import-ready file, plus organised return of your source documents.

Not included

No bookkeeper keeps your books on this plan: categorising, reconciling and closing stay with you, in your own file. GST/HST filing, payroll and year-end preparation are not part of the subscription — they belong to the done-for-you service.

Support

Email support on using your files. Human bookkeeping support is a service, and it is priced as one — on the done-for-you plan.

Your books stay yours

The output lands in your own QuickBooks, Xero or Sage 50 file — never locked inside our system. If you cancel, your files and your documents are yours to take with you.

Subscription questions

Answered plainly

Does a person do my bookkeeping on the subscription?

No. The subscription is software: it converts your documents into a structured, import-ready file. If you want a person to keep your books, that is the done-for-you service — see Pricing.

Which programs does the file work with?

QuickBooks, Xero and Sage 50 — the file is prepared for the program you already use, and your books stay in your own file there.

What counts as a document?

One statement, receipt or invoice. The plan covers up to 250 a month; above that, documents are $0.25 each and itemised on your invoice.

When can I start?

The founding list is open now. Paid checkout opens when payment processing is switched on; list members are invited first, in order, and nobody is charged before checkout opens.

What happens if I cancel?

The subscription stops at the end of the paid period. Your imported books are in your own file, and your documents and output files are yours to take with you.
