Catch-Up Bookkeeping

Behind on your books? The backlog is cleared for one fixed fee for a defined scope — agreed in writing before any work starts.

What catch-up typically costs

A fixed fee, quoted in writing before work begins. As a guide, one year of catch-up typically falls between $1,800 and $2,400 and takes about 2–4 weeks, depending on the state of the records.

The backlog

What “behind” usually looks like

Months unreconciled

Bank and credit-card accounts that have not been reconciled for several months — or longer.

Receipts in a pile

Receipts and invoices in folders, inboxes, and shoeboxes, none of them recorded yet.

A deadline approaching

A GST/HST period or a year-end coming up, with books that are not ready for it.

How catch-up works

Four steps, one fixed fee

01

Free review

On the free intro call, your backlog is reviewed: how many months, which accounts, what state the documents are in.

02

One fixed quote

You receive one fixed fee for a defined scope, agreed in writing before work starts. The cost is known before anything begins.

03

Conversion and reconciliation

The backlog is converted and reconciled in your own QuickBooks, Xero, or Sage 50 file, month by month.

04

Stay current, if you wish

Continuing on the monthly retainer is optional, from $550/month + HST, month-to-month — so you can stay current, if you wish.

Monthly bookkeeping after catch-up is from $550/month + HST, month-to-month — see Pricing for the full scope the fee covers.

Catch-up questions

Before you call

How much will my catch-up cost?

It is a fixed fee for a defined scope, quoted after the free review of your backlog and agreed in writing before work starts. As a guide, one year of catch-up typically falls between $1,800 and $2,400 and takes about 2–4 weeks, depending on the state of the records.

Do I have to become a monthly client afterwards?

No. Continuing on the monthly retainer is your choice. The catch-up scope stands on its own.

What do I need to hand over?

Your statements, receipts, and invoices as they are, sent securely. Sorting them first is not required — that is the work.
